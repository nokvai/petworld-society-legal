Request deletion of your PetWorld Society account

App: PetWorld Society · Publisher: NokNokware (for World Pet Society) · Package: com.noknokware.petworldsociety
Last updated: 16 September 2026

This page is the web resource for deleting a PetWorld Society account created in the app published by NokNokware. You can request deletion here even if you have uninstalled the app. You do not need to reinstall PetWorld Society to submit this request.

Request account deletion on the web (no app required)

Email NokNokware at benjaminthethird@gmail.com and ask us to delete your PetWorld Society account and associated data.

Include the email address you used to sign in to PetWorld Society (email/password or Google). We use that to find and delete the correct account.

Send an account deletion request email

We review PetWorld Society account deletion requests and complete them as soon as reasonably possible, typically within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for security, fraud prevention, or legal reasons.

Optional: delete from inside the PetWorld Society app

If you still have the app installed, you may also delete your account there:

  1. Open PetWorld Society and sign in.
  2. Go to Me (settings).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. This permanently removes your account from the community.

This in-app path is optional. The email request above works without the app.

What NokNokware deletes and what we retain

When NokNokware deletes a PetWorld Society account, we delete the profile, registered pets, photos stored for the community, friendships, and chat tied to that account. This cannot be undone.

We may retain limited records required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or payment-review reasons (for example a GCash payment reference already processed) for as long as those obligations last, then delete or anonymize them.
