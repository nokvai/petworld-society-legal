Request deletion of your Inato Karinderya account

App: Inato Karinderya · Publisher: NokNokware · Package: com.noknokware.inatokarinderya
Last updated: 24 September 2026

This page is the web resource for deleting an Inato Karinderya account created in the app published by NokNokware. You can request deletion here even if you have uninstalled the app. You do not need to reinstall Inato Karinderya to submit this request.

Request account deletion on the web (no app required)

Email NokNokware at benjaminthethird@gmail.com and ask us to delete your Inato Karinderya account and associated data.

Include the email address you used to sign in to Inato Karinderya (email/password or Google). We use that to find and delete the correct account.

Send an account deletion request email

We review Inato Karinderya account deletion requests and complete them as soon as reasonably possible, typically within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for security, fraud prevention, or legal reasons.

Optional: delete from inside the Inato Karinderya app

If you still have the app installed, you may also delete your account there:

  1. Open Inato Karinderya and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm. This permanently closes your account.

This in-app path is optional. The email request above works without the app.

What NokNokware deletes and what we retain

When NokNokware deletes an Inato Karinderya account, we remove or anonymize your profile, saved addresses, favorites, and related personal data tied to that account. Stores you owned are closed and marked deleted. This cannot be undone.

We may retain limited records required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, order history, or payment-review reasons for as long as those obligations last, then delete or anonymize them.
