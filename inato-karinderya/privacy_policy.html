Privacy Policy for Inato Karinderya

App: Inato Karinderya · Publisher: NokNokware · Package: com.noknokware.inatokarinderya
Last updated: 24 September 2026

NokNokware ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Inato Karinderya mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone (optional), role (customer, owner, rider).
  • Authentication: email/password or Google Sign-In via Appwrite.
  • Location: approximate GPS when you browse nearby stores or set delivery addresses (with permission).
  • Orders & marketplace activity: carts, orders, delivery status, ratings, favorites, chat related to stores.
  • Store owner data: store profile, menu, subscription payment references you submit for review.
  • Device & ads: if ads are enabled, Google AdMob may collect Advertising ID and related measurement data.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide marketplace, ordering, delivery, and store-management features
  • Authenticate users and secure accounts
  • Show nearby karinderyas and fulfill delivery/pickup
  • Process store approvals and subscription status
  • Improve reliability and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Serve optional ads (Android) when AdMob is enabled

3. Sharing

We share data with service providers needed to run the App, including Appwrite (backend/auth/storage) and Google (AdMob, Google Sign-In) under their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Google policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Location

Location is used to find nearby stores and for delivery addresses. You can revoke location permission in device settings; some features will not work without it.

5. Ads

On Android, the App may show banner, interstitial, and app-open ads via Google AdMob. Ads are not required to use core ordering features. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy linked above.

6. Children’s Privacy

Inato Karinderya is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information as long as needed to operate the marketplace, fulfill orders, review payments, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.

You may request deletion without reinstalling the app. Instructions: delete-account.html

You may also delete your account in the App:

  1. Sign in to Inato Karinderya
  2. Open Profile
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm

8. Your Choices

  • Edit profile information in the App
  • Revoke camera / photo / location permissions in device settings
  • Delete your account as described above
  • Contact us with privacy questions

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
